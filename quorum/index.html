Quorum / five screens

An LLM roundtable.
One memo.

Quorum puts an LLM roundtable of business experts on one decision: finance, strategy, legal, and operations. Each takes a position in writing, and the session ends with a memo you can circulate. Runs locally with your own API key.

01Overview

Bring one decision.

Type the question you need to settle. The session is built around it, with a fixed set of seats and a written record.

The product homepage, with a sample roundtable.
02Context

Brief the table.

Company, industry, headcount, revenue, runway, risk appetite. The experts use these facts instead of guessing.

Company context form, filled in for a sample company.
03Session

Seat the experts.

Choose a preset or pick from 14 roles: finance, strategy, legal, operations, and more. Each seat has a defined brief.

A session with the decision and the table.
04Positions

Let them debate.

The moderator picks the next speaker from the argument, not a fixed order. Each expert ends with agree, conditional, disagree, or abstain, plus a confidence score.

A session in progress: memo and positions.
05Record

Take the memo.

Decision, reasoning, where the table disagreed, risks, owners at 30/60/90 days, open questions, assumptions. Export as Markdown.

The decision record with four written positions.
